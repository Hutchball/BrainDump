## Description

Get thoughts out of your head and into BrainDump. Capture an idea in seconds, then organise it when you're ready.

BrainDump turns your thoughts into colourful tiles on an interactive sphere. Keep track of things to do, books to read, films to watch, websites to explore, and ideas you don't want to lose.

CAPTURE NOW
Tap + and type directly into a tile. Your words save automatically, so you can capture the thought and get on with your day. New thoughts go into Unsorted until you're ready to organise them.

ORGANISE LATER
Open your Brain Dump and choose a category for each thought. Each sorted tile moves out of the queue, leaving the next one ready to organise. Create categories that suit the way you think.

EXPLORE YOUR IDEAS
Rotate your sphere, tap a tile to browse its category, or open a thought to read the full text. Search your library when you know what you're looking for.

KEEP MORE THAN WORDS
Add photos and web links to your thoughts. Send text, images and links to BrainDump from the Share Sheet, or capture through Siri and Shortcuts.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose category colours and customise tile appearance. BrainDump supports Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion on iPhone and iPad.

KEEP YOUR THOUGHTS CLOSE
Capture works without an app account. Store thoughts on your device, use optional iCloud sync across your devices, and export portable backups. Complete thoughts to move them into your archive, or recover recently deleted thoughts for up to 30 days.

START FREE
Use BrainDump free with up to 25 active tiles. Completing or deleting thoughts makes room for more.

GO UNLIMITED WITH BRAINDUMP PRO
Unlock unlimited active tiles with a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. An introductory one-month free trial is available to eligible monthly subscribers. Prices and eligibility are shown in the app. If your subscription ends, your existing thoughts remain available to read and edit.

Monthly subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when the purchase is confirmed, or when an eligible free trial ends. Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.
